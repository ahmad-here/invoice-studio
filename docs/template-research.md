# Template research (Nov 2026)

Goal: choose 10 additional invoice templates that match real demand and are genuinely distinct.

## Sources reviewed
- Adobe Express invoice template categories (photography, freelance, hotel, cleaning, consultation, delivery, contractor, plumber, rent, trucking, catering, hourly…)
- Zoho Invoice template gallery (proforma, freelance, construction, service, contractor, sales, consulting, delivery, medical, hotel…)
- Wave invoice templates (business, construction, contract work, freelancer, graphic design, photography, sales, web development)
- OnlineInvoices.com "USA's most used": construction, hourly, proforma, cleaning…
- Wise / QuickBooks: standard, proforma, timesheet/hourly, past-due
- Invoxa guide: standard, service (hourly), product, recurring, progress, proforma, commercial, credit note
- Quaderno / DHL / Investopedia: proforma and commercial invoice required fields
- LeanLaw, QuickBooks, Square: design practices (clear hierarchy, prominent amount due + payment section, branding, clear line descriptions, PO numbers)

## Findings
1. **Most-repeated industry demand:** construction/contractor, hourly/timesheet, cleaning/home services, medical, hotel, delivery.
2. **Document types are distinct products, not styles:** proforma, tax (GST/VAT), commercial (export), credit note, receipt.
3. **Proforma:** separate numbering, validity/expiry date, estimated total, delivery + payment terms, and a statement that it is not a final/tax invoice.
4. **Commercial invoice:** shipper, consignee, description, HS code, quantity, value, currency, country of origin, Incoterms, mode of transport, declaration.
5. **Tax invoice:** seller and buyer tax IDs, tax per line, tax summary per rate, total in words (common in South Asia and the Gulf).
6. **Design:** amount due and due date must be the most prominent elements; the payment section should be easy to find.

## Templates added
Construction, Hourly Timesheet, Proforma, Tax Invoice (GST/VAT), Commercial (export), Credit Note, Payment Receipt, Medical, Hotel Guest Folio, Home Services.
Industry-specific data (job site, check-in, HS code…) uses custom fields; each template offers its recommended fields in one click.
